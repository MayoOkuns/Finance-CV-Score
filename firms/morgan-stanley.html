The Morgan Stanley interview process

Morgan Stanley is reported as more technically rigorous than some peers even at the HireVue stage, and its UK graduate process includes a distinct numerical test and timed presentation.

  • 01HireVue — 3–5 questions within a 48–72 hour window. Mostly behavioural, but investment banking candidates should expect at least one technical or deal-related question.
  • 02Phone Screen — A shorter follow-up call, often covering resume walkthrough and a couple of light technicals.
  • 03Superday — 3–5 interviews, sometimes including a brief individual presentation or case component, distinct from Goldman's or JPMorgan's format.
