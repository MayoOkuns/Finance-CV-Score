The Goldman Sachs interview process

Goldman Sachs recruiting typically runs through two main stages. Most candidates find HireVue leans heavily behavioural, while Superday is where the technical depth — DCF, financial statements, accounting mechanics — shows up.

  • 01HireVue — a pre-recorded video interview, usually 3–6 questions. Mostly behavioural and motivation-based, with occasional business-sense questions.
  • 02Superday — 3–5 back-to-back interviews in one day. Behavioural questions remain common, but expect real technical depth: DCF walkthroughs, financial statement linkages, and accounting mechanics.
